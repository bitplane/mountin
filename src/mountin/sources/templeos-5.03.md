# TempleOS 5.03

The final TempleOS source snapshot. TempleOS and Terry A. Davis's original
source are dedicated to the public domain. The snapshot includes the complete
HolyC compiler, kernel, RedSea filesystem implementation and distribution
builder.

The checked-in ahead-of-time binaries are retained as historical source-tree
content, but Mountin does not use them as its HolyC bootstrap.

Upstream archive: <https://github.com/cia-foundation/TempleOS>
